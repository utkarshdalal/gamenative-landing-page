On the Xiaomi Pad 7, 319 of 388 games with enough data (82%) are rated Great or Works; 56 are Unreliable and 13 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Xiaomi Pad 7, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
