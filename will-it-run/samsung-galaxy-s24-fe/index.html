Will GameNative run on the Samsung Galaxy S24 FE?

Short answer

Mixed

On the Samsung Galaxy S24 FE, 327 of 463 games with enough data (71%) are rated Great or Works; 100 are Unreliable and 36 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy S24 FE, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 14,850 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 10,932 (74%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
