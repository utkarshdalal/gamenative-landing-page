Most tested games run

On the Retroid Pocket G2, 931 of 1,114 games with enough data (84%) are rated Great or Works; 142 are Unreliable and 41 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Retroid Pocket G2, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
