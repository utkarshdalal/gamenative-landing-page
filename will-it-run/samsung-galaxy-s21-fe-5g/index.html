Will GameNative run on the Samsung Galaxy S21 FE 5G?

Short answer

Mixed

On the Samsung Galaxy S21 FE 5G, 130 of 190 games with enough data (68%) are rated Great or Works; 41 are Unreliable and 19 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy S21 FE 5G, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 4,526 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 3,165 (70%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
