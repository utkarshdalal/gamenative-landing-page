On the Xiaomi Pad Mini, 56 of 77 games with enough data (73%) are rated Great or Works; 11 are Unreliable and 10 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Xiaomi Pad Mini, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
