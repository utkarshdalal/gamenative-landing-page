On the Samsung Galaxy A33 5G, 22 of 50 games with enough data (44%) are rated Great or Works; 15 are Unreliable and 13 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy A33 5G, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
