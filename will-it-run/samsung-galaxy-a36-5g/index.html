Will GameNative run on the Samsung Galaxy A36 5G?

Short answer

Mixed

On the Samsung Galaxy A36 5G, 177 of 268 games with enough data (66%) are rated Great or Works; 76 are Unreliable and 15 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy A36 5G, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
