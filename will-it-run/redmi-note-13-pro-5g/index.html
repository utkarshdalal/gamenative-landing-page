Short answer

On the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G, 129 of 218 games with enough data (59%) are rated Great or Works; 70 are Unreliable and 19 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Redmi Note 13 Pro 5G, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
