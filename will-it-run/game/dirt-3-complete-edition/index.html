Will DiRT 3 Complete Edition run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. DiRT 3 Complete Edition is rated Great or Works on 98 of 113 devices with enough data (87%); it is Unreliable on 13 and Broken on 2.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 6,170 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 5,114 (83%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work

How to play DiRT 3 Complete Edition on Android

  3. Start it from your library. The game runs on the device itself; nothing is streamed.
