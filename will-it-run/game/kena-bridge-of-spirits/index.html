Will Kena: Bridge of Spirits run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Kena: Bridge of Spirits is rated Great or Works on 26 of 30 devices with enough data (87%); it is Unreliable on 3 and Broken on 1.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 1,044 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 889 (85%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
