Will Sovereign Tower run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Sovereign Tower is rated Great or Works on 14 of 24 devices with enough data (58%); it is Unreliable on 9 and Broken on 1.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 1,882 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 1,148 (61%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
