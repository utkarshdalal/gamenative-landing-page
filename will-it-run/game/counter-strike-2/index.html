Will Counter-Strike 2 run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Counter-Strike 2 is rated Great or Works on 48 of 149 devices with enough data (32%); it is Unreliable on 45 and Broken on 56.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 6,281 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 3,625 (58%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work

How to play Counter-Strike 2 on Android

  3. Start it from your library. The game runs on the device itself; nothing is streamed.
