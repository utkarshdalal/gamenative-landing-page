Short answer

It depends on the device. Monster Hunter Wilds is rated Great or Works on 0 of 24 devices with enough data (0%); it is Unreliable on 21 and Broken on 3.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 845 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 567 (67%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
