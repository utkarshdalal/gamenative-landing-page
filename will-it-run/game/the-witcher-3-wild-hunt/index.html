Will The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt is rated Great or Works on 139 of 154 devices with enough data (90%); it is Unreliable on 11 and Broken on 4.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 7,237 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 5,816 (80%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
