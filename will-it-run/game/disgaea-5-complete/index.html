Will Disgaea 5 Complete run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Disgaea 5 Complete is rated Great or Works on 34 of 37 devices with enough data (92%); it is Unreliable on 3 and Broken on 0.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 1,986 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 1,509 (76%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work

How to play Disgaea 5 Complete on Android

  1. Install GameNative on your Android device. It is free, from Google Play or as an APK from gamenative.app.

  3. Start it from your library. The game runs on the device itself; nothing is streamed.
