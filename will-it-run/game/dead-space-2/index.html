Will Dead Space 2 run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Dead Space 2 is rated Great or Works on 19 of 78 devices with enough data (24%); it is Unreliable on 40 and Broken on 19.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 2,859 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 1,163 (41%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work

How to play Dead Space 2 on Android

  1. Install GameNative on your Android device. It is free, from Google Play or as an APK from gamenative.app.
  2. Sign in to Steam, Epic or GOG, whichever store you own Dead Space 2 on, and download it inside the app. You need to own the game.
  3. Start it from your library. The game runs on the device itself; nothing is streamed.

Whether it then runs well depends on your device: check it in the table above first.
