Will STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order™ run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. STAR WARS Jedi: Fallen Order™ is rated Great or Works on 42 of 44 devices with enough data (95%); it is Unreliable on 1 and Broken on 1.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 2,855 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 2,599 (91%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
