Will Celeste run on my phone with GameNative?

Short answer

It depends on the device. Celeste is rated Great or Works on 389 of 451 devices with enough data (86%); it is Unreliable on 55 and Broken on 7.

Each device counts once, however many people use it, and only devices where the game was run at least 5 times are counted. People tend to try a game on hardware they expect to cope, so weaker phones are under-represented here.

Based on 37,583 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 31,284 (83%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
