Sign in to Steam, Epic or GOG, whichever store you own Blasphemous on, and download it inside the app. You need to own the game. 3. Start it from your library. The game runs on the device itself; nothing is streamed.
What does "playable" mean here?
A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth.
