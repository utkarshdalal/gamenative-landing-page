Short answer

Most tested games run

On the HONOR Magic V2, 77 of 96 games with enough data (80%) are rated Great or Works; 15 are Unreliable and 4 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the HONOR Magic V2, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
