On the Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+, 102 of 140 games with enough data (73%) are rated Great or Works; 27 are Unreliable and 11 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE+, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
