Short answer

Most tested games do not run reliably

On the Oculus Quest 2, 51 of 106 games with enough data (48%) are rated Great or Works; 26 are Unreliable and 29 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Oculus Quest 2, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 5,001 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 1,537 (31%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
