Will GameNative run on the Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE?

Short answer

Mixed

On the Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE, 82 of 123 games with enough data (67%) are rated Great or Works; 26 are Unreliable and 15 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
