Most tested games run

On the Lenovo TB321FU, 1,121 of 1,305 games with enough data (86%) are rated Great or Works; 136 are Unreliable and 48 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Lenovo TB321FU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
