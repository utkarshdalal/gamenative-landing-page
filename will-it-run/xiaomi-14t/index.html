On the Xiaomi 14T, 102 of 156 games with enough data (65%) are rated Great or Works; 31 are Unreliable and 23 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Xiaomi 14T, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
