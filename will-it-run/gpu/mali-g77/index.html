Will GameNative run on the Mali-G77?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Mali-G77 GPU, 549 of 876 games with enough data (63%) are rated Great or Works; 227 are Unreliable and 100 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Mali-G77 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 27,757 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 17,844 (64%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
