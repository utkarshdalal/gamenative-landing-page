Will GameNative run on the Adreno 750?

Short answer

Most tested games run

On devices with the Adreno 750 GPU, 4,086 of 4,895 games with enough data (83%) are rated Great or Works; 569 are Unreliable and 240 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 750 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 356,015 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 286,937 (81%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
