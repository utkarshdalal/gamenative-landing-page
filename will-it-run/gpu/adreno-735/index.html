Will GameNative run on the Adreno 735?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Adreno 735 GPU, 955 of 1,275 games with enough data (75%) are rated Great or Works; 231 are Unreliable and 89 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 735 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 48,816 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 36,853 (75%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
