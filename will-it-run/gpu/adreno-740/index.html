Short answer

Most tested games run

On devices with the Adreno 740 GPU, 10,443 of 12,277 games with enough data (85%) are rated Great or Works; 1,394 are Unreliable and 440 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 740 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 3,042,368 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 2,523,016 (83%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
