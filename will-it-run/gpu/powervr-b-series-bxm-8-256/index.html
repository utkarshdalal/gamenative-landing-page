Will GameNative run on the PowerVR B-Series BXM-8-256?

Short answer

Most tested games do not run reliably

On devices with the PowerVR B-Series BXM-8-256 GPU, 123 of 330 games with enough data (37%) are rated Great or Works; 140 are Unreliable and 67 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the PowerVR B-Series BXM-8-256 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 7,318 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 3,451 (47%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
