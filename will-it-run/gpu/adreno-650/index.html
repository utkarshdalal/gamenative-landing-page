Will GameNative run on the Adreno 650?

Short answer

Most tested games run

On devices with the Adreno 650 GPU, 4,424 of 5,612 games with enough data (79%) are rated Great or Works; 855 are Unreliable and 333 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 650 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 492,441 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 388,945 (79%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
