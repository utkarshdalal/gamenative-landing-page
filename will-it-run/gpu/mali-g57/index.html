Will GameNative run on the Mali-G57?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Mali-G57 GPU, 1,596 of 2,864 games with enough data (56%) are rated Great or Works; 801 are Unreliable and 467 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Mali-G57 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 155,557 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 96,717 (62%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
