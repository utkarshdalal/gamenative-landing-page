Most tested games do not run reliably

On devices with the Maleoon 910 GPU, 6 of 58 games with enough data (10%) are rated Great or Works; 22 are Unreliable and 30 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Maleoon 910 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
