Will GameNative run on the Adreno 630?

Short answer

Most tested games do not run reliably

On devices with the Adreno 630 GPU, 85 of 179 games with enough data (47%) are rated Great or Works; 57 are Unreliable and 37 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 630 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 3,842 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 1,946 (51%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
