Will GameNative run on the Mali-G925 Immortalis?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Mali-G925 Immortalis GPU, 803 of 1,195 games with enough data (67%) are rated Great or Works; 292 are Unreliable and 100 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Mali-G925 Immortalis GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 47,151 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 33,995 (72%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
