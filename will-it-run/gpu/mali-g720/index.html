Will GameNative run on the Mali-G720?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Mali-G720 GPU, 715 of 1,201 games with enough data (60%) are rated Great or Works; 312 are Unreliable and 174 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Mali-G720 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 50,188 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 27, 2026, of which 32,879 (66%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
