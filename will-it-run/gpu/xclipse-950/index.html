Will GameNative run on the Xclipse 950?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Xclipse 950 GPU, 195 of 287 games with enough data (68%) are rated Great or Works; 65 are Unreliable and 27 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Xclipse 950 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
