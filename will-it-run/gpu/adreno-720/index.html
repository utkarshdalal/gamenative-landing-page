Will GameNative run on the Adreno 720?

Short answer

Mixed

On devices with the Adreno 720 GPU, 317 of 480 games with enough data (66%) are rated Great or Works; 125 are Unreliable and 38 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on devices with the Adreno 720 GPU, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 11,362 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 7,490 (66%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
