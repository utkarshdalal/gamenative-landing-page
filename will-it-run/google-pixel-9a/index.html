Will GameNative run on the Google Pixel 9a?

Short answer

Mixed

On the Google Pixel 9a, 242 of 332 games with enough data (73%) are rated Great or Works; 62 are Unreliable and 28 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Google Pixel 9a, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.

Based on 8,664 play sessions in the 180 days up to November 21, 2026, of which 6,335 (73%) were playable. A session counts as playable when the game ran for more than 60 seconds at 15 FPS or better. That is a low bar: playable does not mean smooth. How these numbers work
