On the Xiaomi 12T Pro, 72 of 84 games with enough data (86%) are rated Great or Works; 11 are Unreliable and 1 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the Xiaomi 12T Pro, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
