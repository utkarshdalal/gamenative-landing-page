Mixed

On the POCO X6 Pro 5G, 426 of 678 games with enough data (63%) are rated Great or Works; 157 are Unreliable and 95 are Broken.

These are games people chose to try on the POCO X6 Pro 5G, and the most-played ones are mostly lighter or older games, so this percentage overstates what to expect from demanding new releases. See the popular games that don't run reliably.
